## About JiaJia
- JiaJia (家家, "home") helps international students and workers find a comfortable, fairly priced, move-in-ready room in China — from search to settlement.
- Small founder-led team who found off-campus housing in China themselves. Peer-to-peer, honest, no commission from landlords/agents.
- Active cities: Shanghai and Hangzhou (same package prices in both cities).

## Rent and room prices
- Monthly rent depends on city, neighborhood, room type and your budget. There is no fixed rent price list — the team shortlists rooms that fit your budget after you fill in the form.
- JiaJia has many types of rooms; the team finds the best options based on your budget and preferences.

## How to find a room with JiaJia (5 steps)
1. Fill out the form: linktr.ee/jiajia.roomhunt — the team then contacts you on WeChat to confirm your information.
2. Choose your favorite rooms — the team sends photos and videos of several options until you find one you like. If move-in is more than a month away, the team can send sample room styles first to learn your preferences.
3. Pay the 50% deposit to start room inspection — the team visits the room in person, sends videos, or does a real-time video call.
4. Final room selection — the team helps negotiate price and terms.
5. Secure your room — pay the remaining deposit and advance rent to the local agent to lock the room. Everything is ready when you arrive: just sign the contract and move in.

Timing: the team usually starts searching and viewing real rooms about 1–1.5 months before you arrive in China.

## FAQ
Q1: Who do I pay the rent to? How?
Rent is usually paid to the landlord via Alipay or WeChat Pay. If that's not convenient, JiaJia can receive the payment and transfer it for you, with a receipt.

Q2: When can I book a room, and when do I sign the contract?
You can usually book a room 1–15 weeks before move-in. Most contracts are signed on move-in day after scheduling with the landlord.

Q3: Do I pay rent monthly or every 3 months?
It depends on what you and the landlord agree on — either monthly or every 3 months.

Q4: When do I pay the JiaJia service fee?
First payment: 50% deposit before coming to China, once you decide to use the service. Final payment: the remaining 50% in China, after you have successfully moved into your room.

Q5: What fees do I pay when renting a room in China?
Usually 3 main fees:
- 1 month's rent in advance (depending on the agreement)
- Deposit of 1–2 months' rent (refundable when you move out)
- Local agent fee (usually 50% of one month's rent, paid once)
Example: if rent is 3,000 RMB/month, the agent fee is around 1,500 RMB.

Q6: What is the local agent fee?
It covers the Chinese agent's help finding the room, communicating and handling paperwork with the landlord. Usually half of one month's rent, paid once before signing the lease.

Q7: Do I pay the local agent fee to JiaJia?
No. It is paid separately to the Chinese agent who shows you the rooms, not to JiaJia. It is one of the normal costs of renting a room in China.

Q8: How do I cancel JiaJia's service?
Contact the team as soon as possible. JiaJia refunds 50% of your deposit (the rest covers the work already done).

Q9: What types of rooms does JiaJia have?
Many types — the team finds the best options based on your budget and preferences.

Q10: How long is a rental contract?
- Short-term rental: minimum 4 months — good for a semester or language program.
- Long-term rental: around 1 year — better deal and lower monthly rate, great for studies or work.
